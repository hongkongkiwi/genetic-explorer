## Overview

Genetic Explorer is available under a dual-license model:

1. **Genetic Explorer License** (see LICENSE file)

   - Restrictions on competing managed services

2. **Commercial License** (this document)
   - Required for commercial use
   - Allows hosting as a service
   - Removes competition restrictions

## When You Need a Commercial License

You **must** obtain a commercial license if you:

- ☁️ Offer Genetic Explorer as a hosted service (SaaS/PaaS)
- 🏢 Use Genetic Explorer to provide genetic analysis services to customers
- 💼 Bundle Genetic Explorer with a commercial product
- 🧬 Create a competing genetic data analysis platform
- 💰 Generate revenue directly or indirectly from Genetic Explorer

## When You Don't Need a Commercial License

You **do not** need a commercial license if you:

- 👤 Use Genetic Explorer for personal genetic analysis
- 🎓 Use Genetic Explorer for academic research or education
- 🔬 Use Genetic Explorer in a non-profit research institution
- 🏥 Use Genetic Explorer for internal purposes within your organization (no external users)
- ✏️ Contribute code improvements back to the project

## FAQ

### Can I use Genetic Explorer for my personal DNA analysis?

**Yes!** Personal use is completely free and encouraged under the Genetic Explorer License.

### Can I modify the code for my research project?

**Yes!** Academic and research use is permitted. We encourage contributions back to the project.

### Can I offer Genetic Explorer as a free service to the community?

**Maybe.** If you're not generating revenue and not competing commercially, contact us for clarification. Generally, hosted services require a commercial license regardless of whether they're free to end users.

### Can I use Genetic Explorer in my hospital/clinic?

**Internal use:** Yes, for internal analysis without external patient access.

**Patient-facing:** Requires commercial license if patients access the platform or results.

### What happens if I violate the license terms?

Your rights under the license are automatically terminated. We reserve the right to pursue legal remedies for unauthorized commercial use.

### Can I get a trial commercial license?

Yes, we offer evaluation licenses for qualified organizations. Contact us to discuss.
